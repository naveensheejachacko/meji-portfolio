About

From Thrissur to the classroom.

I'm from Thrissur, studied Computer Science at Calicut and Kerala universities, and started teaching in January 2026.

I teach students in all three years of the BCA programme. My classes lean on live examples: I explain an idea, show it running, and then let students build it themselves in the lab. Having built my own web and mobile apps helps me answer the question students always ask: where will I actually use this?
