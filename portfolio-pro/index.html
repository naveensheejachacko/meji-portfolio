About me

An educator with an engineer's curiosity.

I am currently an Assistant Professor in Computer Science at ECR Institute of Management Studies, Udupi, teaching BCA students across all three years under Mangalore University. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Kerala and a BCA from the University of Calicut.

I teach Database Management Systems, Python Programming, Data Structures, Cryptography and Fundamentals of Data Science, and I run programming labs. I connect every concept to working code, and I support students individually as their class advisor and mentor.

Outside the classroom I build and test web and mobile applications with React, Flutter and Python. Reviewing and debugging code every day has sharpened my attention to detail, and it is a big part of why software quality and engineering interest me.
